Can adding bigrams fix information that preprocessing already deleted?

I put our customer-review example into a small Python demo:

“The product works, but the battery is not good.”

Using scikit-learn’s CountVectorizer, I compared four settings:

• Single words, retained: “not” survives.
• English stop words removed: “not” disappears.
• Single words + bigrams, retained: “not good” becomes a feature.
• Single words + bigrams, stop words removed: “not good” is still missing.

Adding bigrams cannot recover a word that was deleted first.

All nine automated tests pass. This demonstrates preprocessing—not a trained sentiment classifier or an accuracy improvement.
